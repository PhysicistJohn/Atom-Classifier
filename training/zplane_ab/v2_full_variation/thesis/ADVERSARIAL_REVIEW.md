# Adversarial Review of THESIS.md

## Verdict

Partly sound, substantially overclaimed, and mis-ranked. Trust the meta-level move and the cheap diagnostics; treat the ranking, the DSP specifics, and the architecture as unreliable.

WHAT TO TRUST. The core reframe is correct and valuable: after ten interventions across six architecture families all landed on the same confusion, the burden shifts from the model to the observation map, the label granularity, and the inference rule, and the document is right that nobody has systematically tested those. Section 3.2's separation of architectural from data/label/definitional fixes is genuinely honest — better than most such documents — and 3.2(a) (if the simulator does not render distinct h and BT, nothing recovers it) and 3.2(c) (make 37 profiles the native output and the 7-label taxonomy a view over it) are the two most valuable paragraphs in the thesis. The convexity argument in 2.7 is correct. Steps 0 and 1, plus the C3 held-out-parameterization split and the C4 empty-window measurement, are all cheap, correct, and should be done. Several honesty flags (the centroid-vs-separability caveat, the unverified 2h formula, the no-precedent fusion, Kumar's dead-seed warning) are real intellectual honesty and should be credited.

WHAT TO DISCOUNT. The ranking is the weakest part. P1 is placed first on the strength of a single datum (E6) that the thesis itself gives two readings of, that has at least two further readings it never considers (padding/effective-length collapse in the resample-then-crop pipeline, and sps collapse to ~2 destroying pulse-shape detail), and that the thesis simultaneously characterises as a shortcut to be destroyed by C2 — so it cannot function as proof. Before any of Step 2, someone should spend twenty minutes checking the non-padded sample count in the resampled path and whether fs is class-correlated in the corpus metadata; either answer can dissolve the top-ranked cause. P2 is close to disconfirmed by arithmetic the thesis never does. D1+A2 predicts the observed donor/attractor asymmetry backwards given the measured within-class spreads, rests on a two-point dose curve with no intermediate lambda, and is contradicted by the null result of per-sample contrastive repulsion. A1's one claimed non-redundant contribution (dsss vs ofdm via conjugate SCF) is probably invalid for the actual corpus profile, because 802.11b at 11 Mbps is CCK with complex chips, not 11-chip Barker BPSK.

WHAT IS UNIMPLEMENTABLE AS SPECIFIED. Section 2 is closer to a grab-bag than the document admits. Five views, six loss terms, two fusion levels, a learned per-branch gate, a routed arbiter, profile-level multi-prototypes and a new backbone, at 92M params against a shipping 38k model, on a petabyte-scale corpus. At least six internal conflicts are identifiable without running anything: the burst-phase-invariance loss destroys the duty-cycle structure three other components depend on and will pull noise-only BLE windows into the class prototype; profile-level margin loss repels sub-profiles that the coarse loss needs close; View B and View C are circularly dependent for the symbol-rate estimate; the triad arbiter is defined on a 7-way argmax the recommended head no longer emits, and is made redundant by M3 if M3 works; C2 forbids receiver properties that View B explicitly includes; and the Mamba-over-attention argument is refuted by the thesis's own stride-256 stem, which leaves ~1,172 tokens. The learned gate in particular is being asked to be estimated inside 20-way 5-shot episodes, which is a strong instability candidate the document does not discuss.

CEILING ESTIMATE. Not defensible as stated. The lower bound (0.90-0.92 for the current input map) is the more credible half but rests on a 750k-episode plateau measured on the 1D ViT, an architecture that was already 1.5 points worse than the incumbent CNN, while the one run that was still climbing when stopped (the complex CLDNN at 0.857, episode 1200 of 5000) is never entered into the ceiling table at all. Finishing that run is cheaper than any roadmap step and directly tests the headline claim. The upper predictions (0.975-0.985 full architecture; the entire held-out-parameterization column) are extrapolations from single-paper external analogues in other domains, and the stated irreducible floor of 1.5-3% openly contains a term the thesis admits is unmeasured and potentially large.

PRACTICAL READING. Do Steps 0 and 1, build C3 and C4, finish the CLDNN run, and run the three cheap corpus checks the thesis did not think to specify (non-padded length in the resampled path, fs-vs-class correlation, and the actual spreading scheme and symbol rate of the wifi-hr-dsss-11m profile). Those seven items cost about a week and can invalidate P1, P2, D1+A2 and A1 respectively. Do not commit to Step 5 or Section 2's architecture until they come back.

## Strongest claim

Step 0 plus Step 1: audit the generator for genuinely distinct h and BT product, recompute the z-distance diagnostic per sub-profile pair (2 bluetooth x 7 gsm = 14 pairs) instead of per pooled class, measure the empty-window fraction, and then — with the existing 38k model frozen — swap the 7 class prototypes for 37 profile prototypes and add a plain k-NN sensitivity probe. This holds up for reasons independent of the rest of the thesis. It costs hours, requires no retraining, uses labels that already exist, is sharply falsifiable (F2: if k-NN is within noise of nearest-prototype, D1+A2 is dead), and rests on the one piece of mathematics in the document that is unambiguously correct: a nearest-centroid rule and a linear softmax head both induce convex class cells, so only a union of cells or a nonlinear head can produce non-convex class regions. That argument correctly forecloses the obvious alternative ("just use a proper classifier") and it does not depend on any of the contested DSP claims. The per-sub-profile z-distance table in particular is the highest-information-per-hour item in the document, because its three possible outcomes (label problem / representation problem / corpus problem) partition the hypothesis space cleanly, and the thesis is right that it should gate everything else.

## Weakest claim

C1 / Step 5: regenerate the corpus with fixed 10 ms absolute-time windows (+0.02 to +0.05, confidence 0.85 on direction). This should be discounted heavily, because the experiment has effectively already been run and it lost. In the existing resampled pipeline, window duration equals 4096/(2*BW) = 2048/BW, which is independent of fs. That configuration is therefore free of P2's 30x duration spread by construction, and it hands GSM a 10.24 ms window — exactly C1's target — Bluetooth 2.05 ms, and BLE 1.02 ms. It scored 0.847 against native's 0.891, worse for every architecture tried. The thesis's own evidence matrix scores this datum "+" in favour of P2. It is the closest thing in the evidence set to a direct disconfirmation of P2 and of Step 5, and the document never performs the division. Compounding this: Step 5 implies a 100 TB to 1 PB corpus described as "free except for compute," breaks the shipping constraint by the thesis's own admission, and its stated 0.85 confidence is the second-highest in the roadmap. Everything downstream that is priced as a multiplier on Step 5 (the "70x more integration" for the cyclic estimators in M6, and part of Step 4's expected gain) inherits the problem.

## Factual / technical errors

1. The corpus profile is named 'wifi-hr-dsss-11m'. In 802.11b, 11 Mbps is CCK (8-chip complementary codes at 11 Mchip/s, symbol rate 1.375 Msym/s), NOT the 11-chip Barker sequence, which is used only at 1 and 2 Mbps. So the thesis's 'chip-to-symbol cycle-frequency ratio of 11:1 for Barker DSSS' is wrong for the actual profile (it is 8:1), and the thesis lists that ratio as one of the small set of scale-free discriminants that justify the entire cyclic branch (View C, M2, Step 4).

2. Worse: CCK chips are complex (QPSK-valued) complementary codes, not real BPSK. Conjugate spectral correlation is non-zero only for real-valued (BPSK-like) constellations. So the 'conjugate-SCF presence indicator (identically zero for QPSK/QAM/OFDM, non-zero for DSSS-BPSK)' — which the thesis calls 'a hard physics-level separator for the one pair P1 cannot reach' (20 MHz WiFi-OFDM vs 22 MHz HR/DSSS) — probably does not fire for the 11 Mbps CCK profile in the corpus. The one place A1 is claimed to be non-redundant is the place it is most likely to fail.

3. 'Conjugate SCF identically zero for ... OFDM' is too strong for real 802.11/LTE frames. 802.11a/g/n L-STF, L-LTF and the SIGNAL field are BPSK, and LTE PSS/SSS are real-symmetric Zadoff-Chu; these produce non-zero conjugate cyclostationarity. A presence/absence indicator built on this will be noisy on exactly the profiles the corpus contains (wifi6-he-*, lte-*, nr-*).

4. The shift-invariance argument used to exclude a burst detector is a misapplication. |R_x^alpha(tau)| is shift-invariant for the infinite-time cyclic statistic of a persistent cyclostationary process. For a burst at random offset inside a fixed finite window, the finite-sample estimate is NOT shift-invariant: the number of burst samples inside the window varies, the burst can be clipped, the effective integration length and in-window SNR vary with offset, and the estimator is biased by the on/off gating. The claim that 'the cyclic branch dissolves the alignment problem' is therefore unsupported, and it is the sole positive argument for excluding the AMC literature's standard two-stage pipeline.

5. Conjugate cycle frequencies are not CFO-invariant: they sit at 2*fc + k/T and shift by 2x the carrier frequency offset. The thesis cites 'non-conjugate cycle frequencies at k/T0 are immune to CFO' as a robustness argument in Rank 3, then builds View C's two headline features (conjugate-SCF presence, the 2h conjugate doublet) on conjugate statistics, in a corpus where 75% of samples carry CFO impairment. The doublet *spacing* survives, but presence-detection and absolute-Hz alpha localization do not.

6. 'Hop rate (1600 Hz, 625 us period) and GSM TDMA frame rate (216.7 Hz, 4.615 ms period) are simply unavailable at any sample rate above about 2 MHz.' The 625 us hop period is observable in a 4096-sample window up to fs = 4096/625us = 6.55 MHz. Only the 4.615 ms frame period fails above ~0.9 MHz. The thesis presents this as 'not an opinion, it is division' while getting the division wrong by 3x on one of the two quantities.

7. 'Variable-length input is a non-issue for a linear-time SSM trunk and impossible for quadratic attention' is false — attention handles variable length natively; the issue is cost, not possibility. And the thesis's own Branch A strides by 256 before the trunk, yielding ~1,172 tokens at 300,000 samples, which is well inside attention's comfortable regime. The load-bearing argument for choosing Mamba over attention ('quadratic attention over 300,000 samples is infeasible, so C1 and the backbone choice are coupled') is contradicted by the thesis's own architecture two sentences later.

8. The Gaussian BT product is a dimensionless quantity (B_gauss * T_symbol). It is therefore fully preserved by bandwidth normalization, RMS normalization and DC downconversion. BT 0.5 (BLE) vs BT 0.3 (GSM) changes the normalized power spectrum shape and the IF eye pattern substantially (BT 0.3 spreads ISI over ~3 symbols; BT 0.5 is close to two-level). So section 3.2(b), 'bluetooth-le versus gsm-normal-burst is irreducible in the current representation,' is wrong on its own terms — the thesis names BT product as a residual difference and then declares the pair irreducible anyway. Scale-free structural differences also survive: GSM normal bursts are 148 symbols with a fixed 26-symbol midamble; BLE advertising packets have a fixed 8-bit preamble plus the 0x8E89BED6 access address and different length statistics.

9. M1 checks absolute-bandwidth separation only *within* the triad. Injecting log10(occupied BW in Hz) into a 7-class problem creates new collisions the thesis never considers: LTE 1.4 MHz and NR narrow carriers land on top of Bluetooth's 1-2 MHz, and wideband FM at ~200 kHz lands exactly on GSM's 200 kHz. This is structurally the same failure the thesis correctly diagnosed for the STFT view (E2: fixed one confusion, introduced another), applied to its own top-ranked fix.

10. 'Not fs itself, unless C2 is verified' provides no protection. log10(occupied bandwidth in Hz) = log10(fs) + log10(fractional bandwidth), and the fractional bandwidth is recoverable from the waveform. Any model given absolute bandwidth can reconstruct fs. If fs is class-correlated, View B leaks the shortcut regardless.

11. Compute and storage for C1+C5 are badly understated. 200k-500k samples at 10 ms with fs up to 30 MHz is 10k-300k complex samples each; at complex64 that is roughly 0.1-2.4 MB per sample, i.e. a 100 TB to 1 PB corpus. 'Regeneration is free except for compute, which is unconstrained by the terms of this thesis' is not a defensible statement about a petabyte-scale artifact. The SSCA estimate ('a few hundred MFLOPs per sample' at N=300,000) is also roughly an order of magnitude low for a strip-spectral-correlation analyzer at useful alpha resolution.

## Logical gaps

1. E6 is circular and it is the only unique support for Rank 1. The thesis argues native>resampled proves absolute scale is worth points (M1, 'the axis on which your one representation-level experiment already produced a 4.4-point effect'), and simultaneously says the native advantage comes from sps, which is 'a receiver-side shortcut' that C2 exists to destroy. A quantity cannot be both the empirical proof of the diagnosis and an artifact to be eliminated before the diagnosis can be tested. If C2 is done first, as Step 2 requires, E6's 4.4 points is expected to vanish, and P1 loses its only '+++'.

2. The thesis names exactly two readings of E6 (scale information vs resampling damage) and designs F1 around those two. There are at least two more, both cheaper to check and both fatal to the ranking. (a) Effective-length collapse: the pipeline is 'resample -> crop/pad to fixed length.' Resampling to fractional bandwidth 0.5 means decimating by fs/(2*BW), which for GSM at 30 MHz is 75x. If the resampler operates on the 4096-sample capture, the resampled input contains ~55 real samples and ~4041 samples of padding, which would explain the entire 4.4 points by itself. Nobody appears to have checked the non-padded sample count. (b) sps collapse to ~2: at 2 samples per symbol the Gaussian pulse shape is barely resolved, so resampling specifically destroys the BT-product distinction the thesis elsewhere identifies as the residual BLE-vs-GSM separator, while native keeps sps up to ~110 for narrowband signals. Reading (b) points to a completely different fix (stop decimating to 2 sps; keep oversampling) than the one recommended.

3. P2 and Step 5 are directly disconfirmed by an experiment already in hand, and the thesis never computes it. In the resampled configuration, window duration = 4096/(2*BW) = 2048/BW, which is a function of the class's bandwidth and is *independent of fs*. That means the resampled variant is P2-free by construction (no 30x fs-driven duration spread) and it hands GSM (BW 200 kHz) a 10.24 ms window — precisely the 10 ms that C1 proposes as the fix — plus 2.05 ms for Bluetooth and 1.02 ms for BLE. It lost by 4.4 points on every architecture. The thesis awards E6 a '+' under P2 in the evidence matrix when it is arguably the strongest disconfirming datum for P2 in the whole set, and it prices Step 5 at +0.02 to +0.05 with confidence 0.85 on direction without engaging with it.

4. D1+A2 predicts the observed asymmetry backwards. gsm pools 7 profiles including GMSK plus 8PSK/QPSK/16QAM/32QAM EDGE and has a within-class spread of 2.053. bluetooth pools 2 profiles and has a spread of 1.495. If a mean prototype landing in the gap between modes creates a low-specificity attractor, the *more* multimodal, *wider* class should be the attractor. Instead the narrower, less multimodal class is the attractor and the more multimodal one is the donor. The thesis asserts the A2 mechanism explains 'exactly the observed asymmetry' without noticing that the measured spreads point the other way.

5. E5's dose-response contradicts the geometric story. lambda=0.5 produced literally no change (214/236). lambda=8.0 produced both a large leakage drop and a catastrophic 0.968->0.279 collapse. A smooth geometric mechanism ('pushing the mean out of the gap drags it out of both modes') predicts a graded trade at intermediate lambda. Zero-then-catastrophe is at least as consistent with the repulsion term simply overwhelming the prototypical cross-entropy and destroying the embedding geometry. Calling E5 'a clean, unambiguous fingerprint of D1 + A2' and 'the single most diagnostic result in the whole program' overclaims from a two-point dose curve with no intermediate.

6. E9 is disconfirming evidence for D1, presented as supporting. The corpus regen went 1811 -> 14k across 37 profiles, i.e. it *increased* the number of physically distinct sub-profiles pooled inside each of the 7 labels. Under D1 the triad should have gotten worse. It stayed flat. The thesis rescues this with 'the two effects partially cancel on the triad and add on the rest,' which is an unfalsifiable post-hoc adjustment, and then upgrades the datum to 'a supporting datum for D1, not a neutral one.'

7. The centroid z-statistic is used with two different standards. For dsss (z=2.956) the thesis correctly flags that centroid distance is not classifier separability and demotes the inference. For bluetooth-vs-gsm (z=0.713) it uses the same statistic uncritically to conclude the pair 'genuinely overlap' and builds section 3.2(b)'s irreducibility claim on it. If centroid distance overstates separability it also overstates overlap; the caveat has to cut both ways.

8. The conjunction argument is an immunizing device that contradicts the rest of the document. Section 1.5 says relieving one constraint yields 'approximately zero measured gain' because the others remain binding, and warns that partial implementation underdelivers relative to linear extrapolation. But section 4 prices Steps 1-3 additively (+0.005-0.02, +0.03-0.06, +0.01-0.03 -> 0.93-0.95), and F1 declares the thesis 'substantially damaged' if Step 2 alone yields under +0.03. The conjunction argument pre-explains exactly the outcome F1 nominates as the falsifier. You cannot hold both.

9. The 750k-episode plateau was run on the 1D ViT, which topped out at 0.876 and was already *worse* than the 38k CNN at 0.891. It establishes that particular architecture's ceiling, not the problem's. The thesis leans on it repeatedly as proof the problem is not backbone-limited (section 2.11: 'already falsified in-house by the 750k-episode run'; section 6.3: 'ten independent experiments have already established that number empirically'). Meanwhile the 98k complex CLDNN reached 0.857 at episode 1200 of a 5000-episode run and was *still climbing* — an unfinished run trending toward or past the incumbent is live evidence against the ceiling claim, and the thesis cites it approvingly as 'the best early trajectory of anything tried' without ever confronting what it implies.

10. The evidence-to-cause matrix is self-scored with no stated scoring rule, and several assignments do not survive inspection. E1 (robust normalization) gets '++' for A1, but nothing about median/MAD scaling bears on alpha!=0 computability. E6 gets '+' for P2 when it is disconfirming. E10 ('2D and 1D have structurally different error sets') is close to tautological for any two different representations and discriminates among none of the six causes, yet is scored '++' for four of them. A matrix whose cells the author assigns after choosing the ranking is not independent evidence for the ranking.

11. Asymmetric evidential standards. Burst detection is excluded partly because 'no published paper runs the controlled aligned-versus-unaligned A/B' — an argument from absence of evidence — while the document builds on MAMCA (explicitly flagged single-team), Hou's cascade (explicitly flagged single-paper), Mod-CL (an unreplicated preprint), an unverified 2h formula, and a fusion construction the author states has no precedent whatsoever. The one intervention the AMC literature actually standardizes on is the one held to the strictest standard.

12. P2 and C2 rest on incompatible assumptions about the corpus. P2's worst case (136 us windows) requires GSM to be sampled near 30 MHz. C2's worry is that fs is class-correlated by construction — which, for a sensible generator, would put a 200 kHz GSM signal at a low fs and defuse P2's worst case entirely. The thesis never checks which is true, yet both claims are load-bearing (P2 justifies Step 5; C2 gates Step 2).

13. Multiple recommended components actively conflict. (i) Loss 4 (burst-phase invariance: pull together two disjoint windows of the same emission) destroys exactly the burst-phase, duty-cycle and repetition structure that View B's duty-cycle scalar, View E's envelope on/off channel, and the entire P2/C1 argument depend on; and for low-duty-cycle BLE it will pull noise-only windows toward the class prototype, which is the label noise C4 exists to measure. (ii) Loss 3 (profile-level norm-softmax margin) applies repulsion between bluetooth-classic and bluetooth-le, directly fighting Loss 2 (coarse 7-class prototypical CE) which needs them mutually close. (iii) View B's log10(symbol rate) is 'taken from the dominant non-conjugate cycle-frequency peak' i.e. from View C, while View C's scale-free alpha axis is 'normalized by the estimated symbol rate' i.e. by View B — a circular dependency with no stated resolution. (iv) Level-2 triad routing is defined on a 7-way argmax while section 2.7's head emits 37 profiles and section 3.2(c) recommends making 37 the native output; the routing predicate is then ill-defined. (v) M3 claims profile prototypes 'dissolve' the triad, which would make the triad arbiter redundant, yet both are specified. (vi) C2 says fs is a receiver property to be decorrelated, while View B explicitly feeds 'samples-per-symbol, and the decimation factor the front end applied' — both receiver properties.

14. The irreducible floor of 1.5-3% is asserted while one of its own four rows is 'unknown, measure it (C4); potentially large at 4096 samples.' A floor estimate that contains an admittedly potentially-large unmeasured term is not a floor estimate. This matters more than it looks, because intervention 5 already found direct evidence that near-empty windows exist in the corpus.

15. Section 6.1's arithmetic conflates leakage with error. The '1D ViT counts are 37.9 percent and 35.8 percent' are dsss->bluetooth (217/573) and gsm->bluetooth (220/614), i.e. leakage into *one* class, not total class error, which must be higher. The 70%-of-remaining-error figure is then built by assuming the CNN's total class error is *lower* than a number that was itself an undercount, and it omits bluetooth's own error from the triad's share.

16. Step 1 is described as requiring no retraining and Step 3 as 'no API change, no UX change.' Both presume that profile-level labels exist in the enrollment pool at inference time. In a few-shot metric-embedding product where users enroll their own signals, they will not, and the fallback (unsupervised clustering, i.e. actual IMP/DP-means) is materially more work than 'you have the labels; no clustering needed.'

## Unaddressed evidence

1. The 98k-param complex CLDNN reached 0.857 at episode 1200 of a 5000-episode run and was still climbing. This run was never finished and never appears in the ceiling table in section 6.2, despite being the single most direct test of the claim 'you are already within one to three points of the ceiling of your current observation map, and no amount of architecture will move you past it.' Finishing that one run is cheaper than every step in the roadmap and could falsify the thesis's headline claim outright.

2. The FNO evidence cuts against the thesis's own phase-equivariance narrative. The z-plane FNO (phase-equivariant, modReLU) scored 0.716 and the complex CLDNN (also phase-equivariant, also modReLU) scored 0.857 and rising. If modReLU dead-zones and strict equivariance capped performance, the two should not differ by 14 points. The thesis attributes the FNO collapse confidently to pole migration ('the textbook signature') while the ES-trained variant reached 0.653 with no collapse at all, which is more consistent with the FNO family simply being a weak function class here than with an instability that a stability parameterization would fix.

3. The production CNN already contains an instantaneous-frequency-spread hand feature, and the CLDNN already had a dedicated instantaneous-frequency branch. The triad confusion survived both. M5 presents the IF channel as 'the direct antidote' and 'the one channel that disambiguates precisely the classes the log-magnitude view collapsed' without noting that IF information was already in two of the tested models and did not touch the triad. Its genuine scope is narrower than claimed: fixing cw absorption inside the 2D view only.

4. Intervention 5 found gsm 6th-order cumulant vectors with norms up to 1.15 million versus a median of ~30, explicitly attributed to 'near-silent burst-edge captures.' That is direct measured evidence that near-empty and partial-burst windows already exist in the corpus in non-trivial numbers. The thesis cites intervention 5 only as scale-imbalance evidence for A3, lists the empty-window fraction as 'unknown' in C4, and still asserts a precise 1.5-3% floor.

5. The preprocessing condition of the 2D STFT experiment (native or resampled) is never stated, and the E2 diagnosis depends entirely on it. If the spectrogram was computed on bandwidth-normalized input, every class occupies the same fractional bandwidth by construction, so every class is the same-width ridge and the cw-absorption is a consequence of P1, not of 'magnitude-only representation.' The thesis's claim that 'a GFSK burst is a narrow bright ridge... and cw is a narrow bright ridge' is false at adequate resolution on un-normalized input, where the two differ in ridge width by the time-bandwidth product. This would reassign E2 from A1 to P1 and change the ranking.

6. The dimensionless ratio (symbol rate / occupied bandwidth) already separates the triad without any absolute scale: GSM 270.833k/200k = 1.35, Bluetooth 1M/1M = 1.0, HR/DSSS 11M/22M = 0.5. This is a scale-free discriminant that survives P1 entirely. Its existence weakens the P1 > A1 ranking considerably — the separation is available in the normalized representation and the model failed to find it, which is an A1/estimation story, not a P1 deletion story.

7. The corpus has 63 sample rates and 16 bandwidths. Whether fs is class-correlated is a five-minute query against the corpus metadata, and the answer determines whether E6 is physics or shortcut, whether P2's worst case applies, and whether Step 2 is interpretable. The thesis makes it a prerequisite (C2) and then argues both P1's ranking and P2's severity on unverified assumptions about it.

8. Intervention 4 (per-sample contrastive repulsion across all pairwise triad embeddings, 215/237, exactly null) is treated only as evidence for P1/A1. It is also evidence against A2: if the embedding geometry contained the distinction and only the mean-prototype inference rule were misspecified, per-sample repulsion — which operates on individual embeddings and bypasses the mean entirely — should have moved something. It moved nothing. The thesis dismisses it with 'there is no direction in which to push them,' which concedes the point but does not propagate it to A2's ranking.

9. Intervention 3's baseline is reported as 214/236 and intervention 3's outcome as 145/162, but the thesis's E5 row writes '236 -> 162' and its M4 arithmetic writes '93 gsm->bluetooth errors eliminated' from the STFT result (236 -> 143). Two different interventions are being compared against the same baseline number without noting that they are different models on different runs with different best-checkpoint selection. The 'converts your measured minus-42 into a plus-93' calculation in M4 mixes counts from the 2D ViT (0.850) and the 1D ViT (0.876) as if they were commensurable.
